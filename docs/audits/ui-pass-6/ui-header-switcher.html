Header audit: league switcher

390px frames, three variants. Recommended outlined in cyan.

10. League switcher

Measured on the shell header at 320/360/390/430 with a 55-character league name and 12 saved leagues: the chip is 106-216px wide and always ends in an ellipsis; the menu has no avatars, 11px text, no search, no sample/Sim entries, and was 775px tall with no scroll (last lens options sat under the pill) and 38px off the left edge at 320 (fixed in the header-audit PR). The chip has square corners next to rounded icon buttons; the lens name was gold (D5 fixed).

RECOMMENDEDV1: Bottom sheet with league cards

Shared Sheet: crest, full name (wraps, never ellipsized), platform, teams, record, Power rank; Connect a league and the sample league inside; lens becomes a segmented control. Header chip = crest + short name + lens.
