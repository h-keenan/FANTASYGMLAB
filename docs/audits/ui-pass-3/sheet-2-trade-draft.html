Trade Analyzer inlines big forms (1110 lines, 41 inline-style blocks) and the multi-team builder is not on shared templates. Goal: one builder that uses TradeCompactCard + compact player cards and the fairness meter.

RECOMMENDEDV1 Two-column scale

Give and Get sit side by side as compact player cards; the fairness meter lives under them and updates live.

Trade-off: 3 assets per side gets tall on a 390px phone; tied to the compact card height.
