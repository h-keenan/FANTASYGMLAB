FantasyDesk information architecture: phone mockups

Companion to docs/audits/2026-11-information-architecture.md. 390px frames, dark liquid-glass, tokens copied from web-next/src/app/globals.css (cyan accent, gold action, glass-surface cards, pill tab strip). Nothing here is a new design language. Every "before" frame is rebuilt from the current components (labels and order are real; heights are approximate). Every "after" frame obeys the rules in section 2 of the doc: one tab row, one control row, compact modules that expand on tap, stat chips under the name.

Reading key: gold = the one primary action; cyan = selected/interactive; a trailing › on a module means it expands in place (or opens a sheet) on tap. Fantasy Sim frames use no "Value" or "Trade value" label anywhere; chips are PROJ, OVR and ADP.

1. Fantasy Sim draft room

Before: a ~400px on-the-clock card, then four stacked control rows, with PROJ/OVR/ADP chips pushed to the right of each row. After: a 56px on-the-clock bar, a recent-picks strip and a mini draft board that are always visible, one tab row, one control row, chips under the name.
