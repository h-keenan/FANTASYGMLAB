# Founder Beta Launch Marketing Kit

External distribution kit for FantasyGM Lab Founder Beta.

**Rollback boundary:** presentation/marketing assets and docs only. No football, rankings, valuations, Trust, auth, entitlements, Stripe, Supabase, Sleeper, lifecycle, navigation, or experimental-flag behavior changes.

**Brand:** FantasyGM Lab brand mark (Command Plate geometry) only. No Candidate A/B/C language, alternate logos, or new marketing palettes.

## Canonical positioning

**Positioning line (keep):**

> League-aware recommendations for dynasty managers who want a clear next move.

**Audit:** Matches the product today — Sleeper league import, Today's Game Plan, Trade Hub, Waivers, and Player Quick View share one recommendation source of truth scoped to the imported league. Do not invent competing taglines.

**Short descriptor:**

> Fantasy football front-office software for dynasty managers.

Aligned with in-product `PRODUCT_TAGLINE` (“Your fantasy football front office”).

## Founder Beta explanation (canonical)

> Founder Beta is early access to FantasyGM Lab while I work directly with the first group of dynasty managers to improve the product.

Avoid wording that implies a finished product, guaranteed uptime, or permanent feature permanence.

## Trust / methodology (short)

> Advice is league-aware, scoring-aware, and roster-context-aware — so recommendations stay consistent across Today's Game Plan, Trade Hub, Waivers, and Player Quick View.

Lead with outcomes. Do not expose implementation jargon. Avoid “AI-powered” unless it specifically helps a reader; the product lead is the decision workflow, not a model pitch.

### Experimental disclosure rules

- Decision Memory, GM Targets, and Share Recommendation default **off** (`DYNASTYGM_EXPERIMENTAL_*`).
- Marketing must say **when enabled** / label **[EXPERIMENTAL]**, or omit them from primary pitches.
- Do not market flag-off functionality as generally available.
- Share cards and Decision Memory / GM Targets feature images must carry the Experimental label.

## Free vs Premium language (#161)

**Principle:** Free should sound useful. Premium is deeper — not “the real product.”

| Free | Premium |
| --- | --- |
| League-aware Game Plan | Deeper recommendation inventory |
| Limited decision inventory | Full trade & waiver boards |
| Player context / ranks | Advanced roster decision depth |
| Core trade / waiver workflows | Decision Memory *where enabled* [EXPERIMENTAL] |
| Session What Changed | GM Targets *where enabled* [EXPERIMENTAL] |
